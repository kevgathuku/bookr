Aggregators

Win: a stranger's platform books your seats — within allotment, idempotently, billed to them, never touching member passes.

Steps

  1. Separate router POST /api/v1/aggregators/<slug>/bookings with per-partner hashed tokens (rotatable) + throttling. Enforce min(allotment, free seats). Log every inbound call for reconciliation.
  2. Idempotency on (aggregator, external_id): retries return the original. Their late cancels use the contract cutoff_min, billed to the aggregator account.
  3. Outbound: read-only schedule feed + HMAC-signed change webhooks with Celery backoff retries.

Deliverables — check them off

Primary source

DRF throttling + authentication docs — scoped tokens and rate limits, the channel's guardrails.
