Credit ledger — MVP done

Win: every credit movement is auditable, races can't double-spend — and with this lesson the MVP is shippable.

Steps

  2. spend_credit locks the chosen pass, spends oldest-valid-first covering the class type, unlimited passes first. refund_credit, reason-required adjust_credit, nightly expire_passes writing expiry rows.
  3. Replace the lesson-4 stub: book_seat calls spend_credit; cancel_booking refunds or forfeits per lesson-5 rules.
  4. Expose GET /api/v1/me/passes plus the ledger view (members see own, frontdesk sees the gym's).
