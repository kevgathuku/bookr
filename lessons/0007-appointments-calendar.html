Appointments and calendar

Win: bookable humans and rooms behind one fast calendar endpoint.

Step 1 — No new app, then availability models

Appointments extend scheduling — no new app. Models: Resource (room/equipment/teacher), AvailabilityRule (weekday, start/end, valid range), AvailabilityException (one date: closed or special hours), Appointment (member, teacher, optional resource, window, status). Rules for teachers live on a Resource(type=teacher) row pointing at the same person — one mechanism, not two.

Cross-table clashes (appointment vs that teacher's classes) can't live in a DB constraint — check them in the booking service before confirming. Appointments use the 24h late-cancel window.

Step 3 — One calendar endpoint

GET /api/v1/calendar?from=&to=&teacher=&room= merges classes + appointments into event objects, bounded to 31 days, prefetch_related, paginated by day. Django nuance: select_related JOINs single-valued relations; prefetch_related is for reverse and many-valued ones — reaching for the wrong one is the classic N+1 source. Render with FullCalendar — backend just returns events fast. Check: week view on seed data under 200ms; overlapping appointment for one teacher → IntegrityError; back-to-back with booking_buffer_minutes=10 → conflict.
