Appointments and calendar

Win: bookable humans and rooms behind one fast calendar endpoint.

Steps

  1. Resource, AvailabilityRule (weekly), AvailabilityException (one-offs), Appointment. Slots are computed — generate_slots minus exceptions minus existing commitments — never stored.
  2. Same exclusion constraints for (gym, teacher) and (gym, resource); buffer_min pads both sides of conflict checks. Appointments use the 24h late-cancel window.
  3. GET /api/v1/calendar?from&to&teacher&room merges classes + appointments, bounded to 31 days, rendered with FullCalendar. Fast by construction: prefetch_related, paginate by day.
