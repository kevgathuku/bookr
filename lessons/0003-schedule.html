Schedule that can't double-book

Win: staff publish classes, and the database itself refuses overlapping room or teacher bookings.

Steps

  1. Models: ClassType, Room, ScheduledClass (status: scheduled/cancelled/completed), append-only TeacherSubstitution. Capacity resolves override → room → type default; shrinking capacity never ejects confirmed bookings.
  2. The important line: Postgres ExclusionConstraint on the class time range per (gym, room) and (gym, teacher) while active. Read the sqlmigrate output by eye — this constraint saves you at 6pm on Monday.
  3. API: bounded GET /api/v1/classes?from&to (max 31 days, select_related), staff-only writes. Class cancellation sets cancelled and fires a stub fan-out task (real refunds/promotion land in lesson 5).
