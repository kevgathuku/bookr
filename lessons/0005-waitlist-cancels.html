Waitlist and late cancels

Win: cancellations heal themselves — FIFO promotion, held seats, and late cancels that cost a credit.

Step 1 — Cancel, then promote

Waitlist order derives from booking creation time — no stored counters to corrupt. cancel_booking(member, booking, by_staff=False) runs in a transaction, then enqueues an idempotent Celery task (keep it in bookings/tasks.py — Celery's autodiscovery only looks in installed apps' tasks modules):

@shared_task
def promote_waitlist(class_id):
    klass = ScheduledClass.objects.get(pk=class_id)
    nxt = Booking.objects.filter(
        scheduled_class=klass, status="waitlisted").order_by("created_at").first()
    if nxt and free_seats(klass):  # re-check inside the task: idempotent
        nxt.status = "confirmed"
        nxt.save()  # hold enforced by a beat sweeper: unclaimed past
                      # waitlist_hold_minutes → seat passes to the next waiter

…next in line. Same task serves cancels, no-shows, and capacity increases — one path, not three.

Step 2 — Late-cancel policy

Compare cancel time against venue.late_cancel_hours: outside → cancelled + refund (call the ledger stub from lesson 4 and log it — real money moves in lesson 6); inside → cancelled_late, credit forfeited, strike counted. Staff override always refunds with a logged reason. No-shows: frontdesk POST …/no_show within 2h after end → forfeit; 3 strikes/30d flags the member (no auto-ban in v1).
