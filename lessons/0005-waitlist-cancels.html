Waitlist and late cancels

Win: cancellations heal themselves — FIFO promotion, held seats, and late cancels that cost a credit.

Steps

  1. Waitlist order derives from booking creation time — no stored counters to corrupt. cancel_seat() transacts, then enqueues idempotent promote_waitlist (Celery). Promoted seats are held hold_ttl_min; expiry passes to the next in line. Same path serves cancels, no-shows, and capacity increases.
  2. cancel_booking: outside the window → refund; inside → cancelled_late, credit forfeited, strike counted. Staff override always refunds with a logged reason. (Ledger calls are stubs until lesson 6 — log them.)
  3. No-shows: frontdesk marks within 2h after end → forfeit; 3 strikes in 30 days flags the member (no auto-ban in v1).
  4. Notifications via a pluggable backend (console now, mailpit here): class-cancelled, promoted, late-cancel receipt.
